Privacy Policy — Photo Resize

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy explains what data Photo Resize ("the app") collects, how it is used, and who it is shared with. We wrote it to describe exactly what the app does — not boilerplate.

Your photos never leave your device

Photo Resize processes every photo entirely on your device. Importing, resizing, cropping and compression all happen locally, using only Apple's own on-device frameworks. We do not upload, transmit, or have any server to store your photos on. No photo, or any data derived from a photo, is ever sent to us or to any third party.

To import photos, the app uses Apple's system photo picker, which does not grant the app ongoing access to your photo library — only the specific photos you choose. If you save a resized photo back to your library, the app requests permission to add photos only (not to read or manage your existing library).

Advertising

Free-tier use of the app is supported by banner ads served through Google's AdMob SDK. If you purchase "Remove Ads," ads (and this data collection) stop entirely for your device.

While ads are shown, Google's SDK may collect and process:

  • Device and advertising identifiers
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Basic device and app information (device model, iOS version, app version)
  • Information about how you interact with ads (impressions, taps)
  • Technical data used to detect crashes, errors, or invalid/fraudulent activity

Whether ads are personalized to you depends on the permission you give:

  • Tracking permission (iOS App Tracking Transparency). The app asks for this permission once. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized using cross-app tracking. You can change this choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • If you're in the EU/EEA or UK, a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) asks separately for your consent to ad personalization, under GDPR. You can change this choice at any time from Settings → Privacy Options inside the app.

Google acts as an independent data controller for the data its SDK collects — meaning Google, not us, decides how that data is used and is responsible for it. Google is bound by its own privacy policy and advertising data-protection terms to protect this data at least as well as described here; we share no other data with anyone. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services.

Purchases

The app offers one in-app purchase, "Remove Ads," handled entirely by Apple's App Store (StoreKit). We never see your payment details. Your purchase status is checked against Apple's own records each time the app launches — we do not run our own purchase or receipt server.

What we do NOT do

  • No account or sign-up — the app has no login, and we never ask for your name or email.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs of our own — the only third-party code in the app is Google's ad SDK, described above.
  • No data sold to anyone.
  • No data about you stored on any server we operate — we don't operate one.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a small set of preferences locally on your device, such as your default resize preset, export format, quality settings, light/dark appearance choice, and whether you've seen the one-time photo-access explainer. This data stays on your device, is not personally identifiable, and is not sent anywhere by us. (Your "Remove Ads" purchase itself is remembered by Apple's App Store, not by us — see Purchases above.)

Smart Crop

Smart Crop uses Apple's on-device Vision framework to find faces and subjects when choosing how to crop a photo. This analysis runs entirely on your device; the result is used only to frame that one crop and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Data retention and deletion

We retain nothing — we have no servers to retain anything on. Deleting the app deletes every preference it stored. Data Google collected while showing you ads is retained under Google's own retention policy. To stop or revoke data collection: decline or revoke Tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, use Settings → Privacy Options in the app (EU/EEA/UK) — or simply purchase "Remove Ads" to stop ad-related data collection entirely.

Your rights (GDPR)

For any data this app collects directly, there's nothing to request — we don't hold any (see above). For data Google's SDK collects while showing ads, Google is the controller and you can exercise your rights — access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent — directly with Google, or through the controls described in "Data retention and deletion" above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority. The legal basis for ad personalization is your consent (given through the Privacy Options form); non-personalized ads and fraud prevention rely on Google's legitimate interest. Google may process this data outside the EEA/UK under its own data-transfer safeguards.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell personal information. Personalized ads from Google may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law. You can opt out by declining Tracking permission (iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking) and through Google's ad settings.

Children's privacy

Photo Resize is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from children. The advertising described above is subject to Google's own policies for child-directed treatment where applicable.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how your data is used, we'll say so in the app, and may ask for your consent again where the law requires it.
